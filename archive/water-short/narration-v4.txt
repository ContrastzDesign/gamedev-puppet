[slower and softer] Then the camera goes under. The light changes. The sound gets muffled. Suddenly, you feel like you’re underwater too.

[curious] Even those little droplets you sometimes see on the screen? The game draws them right over the picture.

[warm and deliberate] It’s a whole stack of tiny tricks. Put them together, and you’ll hold your breath without even realizing it.
