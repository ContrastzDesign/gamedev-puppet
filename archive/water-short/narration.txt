The water you see in games isn’t real.

Think about the last time you went swimming in GTA. Your character dives in, water splashes everywhere, and somehow your screen is still dry.

So how does the game make it feel like you went underwater?

First, it draws a surface that ripples and catches the light. When your character jumps in, the game adds a splash and switches them to a swimming animation.

Then the camera goes under. The light changes. The sound gets muffled. Suddenly, you feel like you’re underwater too.

Even those little droplets you sometimes see on the screen? The game draws them right over the picture.

It’s a whole stack of tiny tricks. Put them together, and you’ll hold your breath without even realizing it.
