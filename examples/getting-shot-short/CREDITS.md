### Intended use in the short
- **thud** — bullet hit / impact
- **grunt** — effort vocalization on hit
- **breath** — heavy breathing as health drops
- **heartbeat** — tension loop under damage beat
- **whoosh** — soft camera whoosh
- **pop** — placard / UI soft pop
- **ding** — mint solved tick
- **sparkle** — shine / magic twinkle accent
- **muffled-hit** — optional dull/muffled hit layer
- **click** — respawn / UI button click
