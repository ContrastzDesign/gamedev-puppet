The biggest games, 2000s to 2011
Which games earned the most in the United States in the early 2000s, late 2000s, and early 2010s? Here’s one annual sales leader from each period, ranked by tracked full-game dollars.
Number one, 2002. Grand Theft Auto: Vice City led the annual chart, ahead of Madden NFL 2003. An open-world action game took the top spot.
Number two, 2007. Guitar Hero III: Legends of Rock ranked first, ahead of Madden NFL 08. Its guitar-controller bundle matters here because a higher-priced bundle can generate more dollars per sale.
Number three, 2011. Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3 finished first. The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim was second. Across these three years, three different genres led the same US sales chart.
