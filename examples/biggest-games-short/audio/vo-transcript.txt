The biggest games, 2000s to 2011. Which games earned the most in the United States in the early 2000s, late 2000s, and early 2010s? Here's one annual sales leader from each period, ranked by tracked full game dollars. Number 1 – 2002 Grand Theft Auto Vice City led the annual chart ahead of Madden NFL 2003. An open world action game took the top spot. Number 2 – 2007 Guitar Hero 3 Legends of Rock ranked first ahead of Madden NFL 08. Its guitar controller bundle matters here because a higher priced bundle can generate more dollars per sale. Number 3 – 2011 Call of Duty Modern Warfare 3 finished first. The Elder Scrolls 5 Skyrim was second. Across these three years, three different genres led the same US sales chart.
