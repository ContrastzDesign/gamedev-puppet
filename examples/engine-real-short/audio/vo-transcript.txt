Which engine looks the most real? Which game engine comes closest to real -world footage? Two real -time demos show what developers can achieve, and one production constraint explains the difference. Number one, Unreal Engine 5 for a city at street level. In The Matrix Awakens, Nanite handled highly detailed geometry and Lumen calculated lighting. The player could move through the city while the engine rendered it. Number two, Unity for a close -up face. Its enemies demo used the high -definition render pipeline, detailed eye and skin shaders, and simulated hair. Those details matter because the face fills the frame. Number three, the frame budget. At 60 frames per second, an engine has about 16 .7 milliseconds to draw each frame. A realistic city and a realistic face spend that budget on different work. Both engines can produce photorealistic shots.
