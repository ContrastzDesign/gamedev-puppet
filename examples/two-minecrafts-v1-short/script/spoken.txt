Why Are There Two Minecrafts?
Say your redstone farm works on Java. Then you open Bedrock, and it breaks. But why did they even make two versions?
And why not just merge them into one?
Well Java is the original code minecraft was written in but doesn't run on consoles or phones, so Bedrock was built in another programming language.
Now every feature gets built twice, and both have to match. This is what you call parity.
Now if you merge them, millions of worlds and mods stop working. So you'd break the thing players love most.
This is why the comments are still fighting about it.
It's one game on the box. But It's two games in the code.
