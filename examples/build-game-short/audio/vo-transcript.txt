where should you build your game? What's the best platform for making a game? Number one, Roblox Studio. By far the easiest platform to get started on. You can basically get everything for free, but it isn't as easy to publish your game. You still have to pay publishing fees on any platform you put your game on. And depending on how big your game gets, it can get pretty expensive. Number three, Nilo. For a playable 3D world in a browser, even non -developers can describe a game, edit it, and publish a link.
